Research totals are the full prereq closure of the drive and its reactor, with shared prereqs counted once. Package mass is the drive plus a power plant sized to its draw (draw × the plant's t/GW; the plant chosen is the cheapest to research that is of the class the drive demands and can carry the load) plus Tin Droplet radiators for the waste heat the plant does not convert — (1 − plant efficiency) × drive draw, at 8 kW/kg. Open-cycle drives radiate through the nozzle and carry none. Propellant is the rocket equation over the hull mass and Δv you set — dry mass is your hull plus the propulsion package, and acceleration is quoted with full tanks. The tanks themselves, and the hull, are not massed. Alien drives are left out — they arrive from captured hulls, not from research. Drives are plotted as their x6 variants — what ships actually mount — except the Orion-type drives, which only exist as x1. Bold entries in the brackets below match or beat the Helicon Drive on both axes.
