Research totals are the full prereq closure of the drive and its reactor, with shared prereqs counted once. Package mass is the drive plus a power plant sized to its draw (draw × the plant's t/GW); the plant chosen is the cheapest to research that is of the class the drive demands and can carry the load. Radiators, tanks, hull and payload are not counted. Alien drives are left out — they arrive from captured hulls, not from research. Drives are plotted as their x6 variants — what ships actually mount — except the Orion-type drives, which only exist as x1. Bold entries in the brackets below match or beat the Helicon Drive on both axes.
