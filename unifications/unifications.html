Claims belong to a nation, not to whoever holds its land — unify a nation and its claims die with it. Phases are therefore ordered deepest-first: every nation spends its own claims before it is swallowed. Research totals are the full prereq closure, with shared prereqs counted once. Dashed project tags arrive from events and cannot be researched. Rows marked FORM FIRST belong to nations that hold no land at the Broken Earth start. Launder hostile claims counts the conquer–wait–release–unify trick: a hostile claim on a capital cools into a peaceful one if you hold the region long enough, so the nation can be released, spend its own claims, and then be unified whole. The waiting is a random roll across every hostile region you hold, so all the laundering is done first, in the opening phases, and the unifications it enables land much later. It is off by default — every step costs a war and an open-ended wait.
